What the video specialists in this seat have learned working on Pinnacle AI.
- The old textbook name (Beehive, old NCERT Science, the four-book SST set) is gone for Class 9 as of this academic year — any future Class 9 content work has to check against Ganita Manjari, Exploration, Kaveri and Understanding Society: India and Beyond, not the pre-2026-27 chapter names still floating around most YouTube search results.
- Multiple independent sources (tiwariacademy, competishun, educart, learncbse) agree closely on the new chapter lists, so cross-checking 3+ sources before touching a curated topic label is the way to avoid shipping a wrong chapter name — that's the one defect this codebase treats as worst-in-class.
