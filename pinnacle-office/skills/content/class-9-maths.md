# class 9 maths

What the content specialists in this seat have learned working on Pinnacle AI.
- CBSE Class 12 Economics (030) is NOT Micro+Macro — it's Part A Introductory Macroeconomics (5 units, 40 marks) + Part B Indian Economic Development (3 units, 40 marks). Unit 6 now merges the old 'Development Experience 1947-90' and 'Economic Reforms since 1991' chapters into one. Unit 7 'Current Challenges' is the single highest-weighted chapter in the whole Class 12 curriculum at 20 marks.
- Chapters 9-19 are the poetry (Flamingo) and Vistas prose half of Class 12 English — each is much shorter than the ch 1-8 prose chapters, so writing a distinct, non-overlapping second question per chapter meant digging for a secondary detail (e.g. a supporting image, a minor character, a causal mechanism) rather than restating the chapter's central theme a second time.
