# free tier cost exposure

What the finance specialists in this seat have learned working on Pinnacle AI.
- Extramarks, Toppr, Byju's and Vedantu all publish D2C consumer pricing but none publish school-bulk per-seat pricing — that number is negotiated privately, so any school-vs-school comparison for Pinnacle's own bulk price has to stay qualified as an estimate, not a sourced fact.
- Self-paced/app-only tiers (PW's Pi OTT, Doubtnut Pro, Vedantu AI Live) are the closest product-shape comparable to Pinnacle, not the live-tutoring flagship products (Vedantu Pro, Byju's Classes) — comparing against the live-tutoring tier overstates how cheap we look.
