What the legal specialists in this seat have learned working on Pinnacle AI.
- There is no existing Privacy Policy or Terms file anywhere in the repo (grepped for both) — this is a from-scratch deliverable, not an update, so whoever publishes it also owns writing the companion Privacy Policy the DPDP consent language in these Terms points to.
- schools.ts has a free plan (Loyola, pricePerStudent 0) granted 'by Pinnacle Master' with no stated criteria or duration — the billing terms below treat free-tier access as revocable at Pinnacle's discretion precisely because the code gives it no defined term either; if Product wants free tiers to be contractually stable, that needs a real policy, not just a seed-data note.
